## 🏛️ Website Structure & Sections

1. **Top Advisory Bar & Sticky Navbar:** Legal emblem, Bar Council accreditation notice, Chambers direct telephone, smooth anchor links, and mobile drawer.
2. **Hero Section:** “Trusted Legal Counsel. Clear Direction. Stronger Outcomes.” with CTAs, trust badges, and chamber showcase.
3. **Trust & Values:** 4 core pillars with line icons: Client-Focused Guidance, Professional Integrity, Transparent Communication, Strategic Legal Solutions.
4. **About the Firm:** Two-column editorial introducing the 1987 heritage, judicial ethics, and direct access to Bar Council roll verification.
5. **Practice Areas (10 Modules):** Interactive service cards with category filters (Litigation, Property, Corporate, Family, Documentation) and deep-dive modal view.
6. **Our Approach:** 4-step client journey (Initial Consultation → Case Assessment → Strategy & Action → Ongoing Communication) with horizontal/vertical timelines.
7. **Legal Team:** Full advocate profiles with live high-resolution Bar Council Certificate Viewer modal.
8. **Why Choose Us:** 6 editorial pillars detailing ethical conduct, confidential handling, and individual attention.
9. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):** Accessible accordion answering consultation scheduling, documents to bring, billing structures, and confidentiality.
10. **Chamber Contact & Consultation Intake:** Two-column layout with chamber address, phone, email, office hours, and a conflict-check consultation form with live validation and reference ID generation.
11. **Final Call to Action:** “Have a Legal Question? Start with a Conversation.”
12. **BCI-Compliant Footer & Modals:** Rule 36 Bar Council of India disclaimer, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Legal Disclaimer modals.
